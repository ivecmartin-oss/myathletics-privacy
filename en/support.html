How can we help?

Practical help for your everyday sport.

Sign-in and password reset

Check your email address and internet connection. If you forgot your password, use the reset option on the sign-in screen, if available. Check your spam folder too. If no message arrives, contact support. Never send us your password or sign-in codes.

User roles and club connections

Athlete, Coach, Parent and Club Manager roles determine access. If expected content or a child connection is missing, first check your club connection with your coach or club manager. Contact support for technical issues.

Notifications

Check the device notification permission for MyAthletics, Focus settings and internet connection. Also check in-app notification settings, if available.

Training, competitions and results

Check the selected date, club and role. Ask the responsible coach or club manager about schedules, invitations, attendance or result corrections. Report technical errors to support.

Contact support

Describe the issue, device model, iOS and app versions, and the steps that lead to the problem. Remove personal information about others and children from screenshots before sending them. No response time has been specified.
