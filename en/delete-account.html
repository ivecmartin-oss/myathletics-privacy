Request account deletion

A clear way to request removal of your account and related personal data.

  1. Send your request. Email myathleticssupport@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. Use the subject “MyAthletics – account deletion request”.
  2. Explain the scope. State that you want your account deleted and, if needed, clarify which related data your request covers. Do not include passwords, sign-in codes or identity documents in your initial email.
  3. Wait for further instructions. Support may need proportionate verification of identity or authority, particularly for a child's account. The exact deletion process and timeframes still need confirmation.

What happens to the data?

The request covers personal account information and applicable user-owned data, subject to applicable legal obligations. Some shared messages, conversations or photos may remain with other recipients. The deleted user's identity may be removed or anonymized.

Automatic deletion and in-app deletion have not been verified. This page provides an email request route; clicking the link does not itself delete an account. Ask support about data you want to keep before deletion.
