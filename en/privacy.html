MyAthletics Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. About this policy

MyAthletics connects athletes, parents, coaches and athletics clubs. This policy explains what personal data we process, why we need it, who can see it and how you can exercise your rights. It applies to the MyAthletics app. The previous policy dated October 30, 2026 remains available in the archive.

2. Controller and contact

The controller of personal data in the MyAthletics app is Martin Ivec, a natural person, developer and operator of the MyAthletics app.

For privacy questions and rights requests, email myathleticssupport@gmail.com. An additional contact or postal address has not been confirmed and remains an open question.

3. Data we process

The scope depends on your role, connections and the features you use:

  • First and last name, email address, sign-in information and user role.
  • Year of birth and sex. New forms request only the year of birth. Older accounts may retain a full date of birth; a year may also be technically stored in a date field.
  • A telephone number retained on an existing account. It is currently hidden in the user interface; hidden data is not necessarily deleted.
  • Connections with a club, training groups, coaches, parents and other users.
  • Sports results, personal bests, medals, disciplines, competitions, training sessions and invitations.
  • Training responses, absence reports and actual attendance recorded by the coach.
  • Profile and shared photos, messages and other content users enter or share.
  • Technical data for sign-in, notifications, operation and security, such as account identifiers, device identifiers for push notifications, and error or access records where generated during use.

You may enter data yourself, or a linked parent, coach or club may enter it within their permissions, for example a result or attendance record. If older profiles contain additional information such as height and weight, you can request access, correction or deletion.

An absence reason or message may also reveal illness or injury. Only include health details if necessary. Processing health data requires an appropriate condition under Article 9 GDPR in addition to an Article 6 legal basis; the applicable condition and any process for obtaining explicit consent remain subject to legal verification. Voluntarily entering information does not itself replace that condition.

4. Why we use data

  • To operate, maintain and secure the app and provide user support.
  • To register users, enable sign-in, manage accounts and provide role-based access.
  • To connect athletes, parents, coaches and clubs.
  • To organise training and competitions, responses, invitations and attendance records.
  • To follow results, personal bests, medals and sporting progress.
  • To enable communication and notifications between authorised users.
  • To improve and develop the app and provide statistical overviews within an individual club.

We use anonymised or aggregated data for development where possible. Aggregated data that still allows someone to be identified remains personal data.

Future statistical features may include additional analysis of results, attendance and progress. This describes possible development, not a promise that every analysis is already available. Access must be limited to appropriately authorised club users. New uses require a suitable legal basis and, where necessary, further information.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising profiling or provide it to other companies for their independent marketing purposes.

5. Legal bases and necessary data

Each purpose must have a valid legal basis. Depending on the circumstances, these may include:

  • Performance of a contract or requested service (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR): for an account and features you request, where processing is actually necessary and there is a valid contractual relationship.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): for security, abuse prevention, fixing errors and proportionate improvements, where processing is necessary and a balancing assessment shows that your rights do not require a different outcome. Children require particular care.
  • Legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)): where a specific law requires processing or retention.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): for optional processing that relies on it; consent must be freely given, specific and informed, and you can withdraw it.

This list is not blanket consent to all processing. The final allocation of bases for individual club, development and child-related processing, and the relationship between the app controller and clubs, still needs to be documented. A club or parent connection does not by itself replace a legal basis.

Without data necessary for registration or a chosen feature, that feature may not be available. An optional photo or additional message is not the same as required account data.

6. Minor athletes and parents

Minor athletes can register their own account and connect with a parent. A parent can also create an account for a child. The technical ability to register does not mean all legal requirements have already been met for every age.

Where an information society service is offered directly to a child and processing relies on consent, Article 8 of Slovenia’s ZVOP-2 sets the age for independent consent at 15; for a younger child, consent must be given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, subject to Article 8 GDPR. This is not a general age threshold for all legal bases. ZVOP-2 text published by the Information Commissioner.

Open legal question: the basis for independent registration of children under 15, and the process for verifying or authorising parental consent, have not been confirmed. If a contract is relied on, rules on a child’s capacity to enter contracts also need to be checked. This policy does not confirm that such a process has been implemented.

7. Who can see data

Connecting with a club

By connecting with an athletics club, an athlete enables the club manager and the coaches appointed by that manager to access data needed for club activities. This includes first and last name, year of birth, sex, sports results, competitions, training and attendance. Access must be limited by role and assignment; the connection is not permission for unrestricted public publication or marketing.

A club may be an independent controller for its own activities. The precise allocation of responsibilities between the club and app controller needs confirmation based on actual processing; you can also ask the club about its activities.

Connecting with a parent

For an athlete with their own account, a linked parent is intended to have limited viewing access, or the Viewer role: following the profile, results, competitions and training. This role is not intended for managing the athlete’s account or viewing their friends and private conversations. A parent who creates an account for a child may have broader management permissions.

Access scope needs verification: the inspected local version of the parent API also returns an email address, telephone number and additional profile fields. Alignment of the actually deployed version with Viewer restrictions is not yet confirmed. We therefore do not guarantee that all these fields are already technically excluded from parent access, nor do we claim that a parent can see all personal data.

Photos and messages

You can set profile photo visibility using the existing privacy options. Shared photos and messages are available to entitled recipients according to actual access permissions. Deleting an account or ending a connection does not necessarily delete content for other recipients; see section 11.

8. Cloud storage and technical providers

Personal data is stored in cloud infrastructure to support reliable app operation, data availability and protection, and to reduce the risk of loss.

Contracted technical providers may process data as necessary for hosting, storage, email and notification delivery, and operational support. Processors may process data only under instructions and within the agreed service; an Article 28 GDPR agreement is required where applicable. Apple services may be used for app distribution and push notifications.

A verified list of named providers, their roles, subprocessors and processing countries has not yet been confirmed. You can request information about recipients and transfers at the contact email address. Describing categories of providers does not confirm that all contractual and transfer requirements have already been met.

9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure and loss. Access is limited according to user roles and connections. No online service can guarantee absolute security; cloud storage is not a guarantee against loss.

10. How long data is kept

Data may be kept for as long as necessary for a specified purpose, taking account of legal obligations and potential legal claims. Criteria include account usage, the type of data, the need for sporting history for entitled recipients, shared content, security needs and statutory periods.

Account data, sporting history, messages, photos and technical records do not necessarily have the same retention period. Backups may remain until the relevant cycle expires; we do not promise immediate deletion from every copy. Specific periods, backup periods and a verified removal process remain open. Indefinite retention simply because a period has not been set is not permitted.

11. Account deletion and shared content

Request account and data deletion at myathleticssupport@gmail.com; instructions are on the Delete account page. Permanent in-app account deletion is disabled in the inspected local code, so we do not promise immediate or automatic permanent deletion using an app button. Signing out, removing the app, unlinking a parent or deactivating a profile does not necessarily permanently delete data.

After verification of a request, data must be removed or anonymised where there is no remaining valid basis for retention. Some data may remain due to legal obligations, legal claims or the legitimate rights of others; reasons for any limitation of erasure must be explained to you.

Shared conversations, photos or sporting history may remain with entitled recipients where continued retention is lawful. Personal links or attribution to a deleted user are removed or anonymised where appropriate and implemented; complete automation of this process is not confirmed. Data may also temporarily remain in backups. The precise scope of retained content and technical timelines still need confirmation.

12. Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you can request access and a copy, correction, erasure, restriction and portability where applicable. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Email myathleticssupport@gmail.com. Proportionate identity verification may be requested to protect your account. Requests must be answered without undue delay, normally within one month; complex or numerous requests may justify up to two additional months, with reasons and notice given in the first month. European Commission guidance on requests.

If you believe your rights have been infringed, you can complain to the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia or another competent supervisory authority.

13. Transfers outside the EEA

Providers may process data in different countries. We do not guarantee that data is stored or accessible exclusively within the European Economic Area (EEA).

Transfers outside the EEA require a valid GDPR mechanism, for example an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, with additional measures where needed. These are possible mechanisms, not a claim that they have already been verified for every provider. European Commission rules on international transfers.

The actual countries, mechanisms used and how to obtain a copy of safeguards still need confirmation. You can request information at the contact email address.

We update this policy when processing, features or legal requirements change. The latest version is on this page. A new incompatible use requires an appropriate legal basis and information in advance; this policy is not advance permission for every future feature.
